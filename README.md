# 🚀 Amazon Business Intelligence & Operational Analytics Dashboard

## 📖 Overview

This project presents a comprehensive **Business Intelligence Dashboard** developed in **Power BI** to analyze Amazon retail operations across multiple business dimensions, including sales performance, profitability, customer experience, operational efficiency, payment methods, and regional performance.

The dashboard transforms raw business data into actionable insights, enabling stakeholders to make data-driven decisions and identify opportunities for growth and optimization.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Monitor sales performance against targets.
* Evaluate profitability and profit margins.
* Analyze customer satisfaction trends.
* Track operational efficiency across regions.
* Identify top-performing product categories.
* Support strategic business decision-making.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

* Power BI

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### Executive Dashboard

Provides a high-level overview of:

* Total Revenue
* Total Profit
* Profit Margin
* Revenue Target Achievement
* Revenue by Product Category
* Yearly Discount Trends

### Operational Analytics Dashboard

Provides detailed operational insights including:

* Sales by Payment Method
* Order Distribution by City
* Customer Ratings
* Department Performance Ratings
* Delivery Performance Metrics

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Financial Performance

* Revenue reached **10.29M** against a target of **14M**.
* Profit generated **2.59M**.
* Profit Margin achieved **25.14%**.

### Product Performance

Top revenue-generating categories:

* Home Appliances
* Books
* Fashion

### Customer Experience

* Customer Rating achieved **4.81/5**.
* Customer Service department recorded the highest satisfaction level.

### Operations Performance

* Significant variations in order volume across cities.
* Opportunities identified for logistics and regional optimization.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Increase sales efforts in underperforming regions.
* Improve digital payment adoption.
* Optimize logistics and delivery operations.
* Expand marketing initiatives in low-performing markets.
* Focus on high-performing product categories.
